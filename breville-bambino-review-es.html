Cafetera espresso manual y compacta

Breville Bambino Reseña

La Bambino mantiene lo esencial: tamaño compacto, calentamiento rápido y control manual de la textura de la leche. Es una opción sensata para empezar a preparar espresso sin pagar extra por el vaporizado automático de leche.

Ideal para: Beginners who want a compact espresso machine and are happy to steam milk manually.

Lo más destacado

Esta reseña se basa en especificaciones publicadas por el fabricante y documentación del producto. Brewcraft no ha realizado pruebas prácticas de laboratorio ni pruebas prolongadas de esta cafetera.

Ventajas

  • ThermoJet system reaches extraction temperature in about 3 seconds, according to Breville.
  • 54 mm portafilter and single- and dual-wall filter basket compatibility.
  • PID temperature control and low-pressure pre-infusion.
  • Varilla de vapor manual gives you control over milk texture and latte-art practice.

Aspectos a considerar

  • Leche steaming is manual, so expect a learning curve if you mainly make cappuccinos or lattes.
  • No built-in grinder; budget for a capable espresso grinder.
  • The 54 mm accessory ecosystem differs from standard 58 mm setups.

¿Para quién es?

Choose the Bambino if you want a straightforward, compact machine and prefer to spend less on the espresso machine itself. If you make milk drinks every day and want the machine to handle texturing, compare the Bambino Plus before buying.
