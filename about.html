About Brewcraft / Editorial standards

Good recommendations start with honesty.

Brewcraft helps home coffee drinkers understand espresso machines, grinders, and the trade-offs behind choosing equipment. Our goal is to make research easier—not to make every product sound perfect.

Useful coffee gear advice should be clear about what was researched, what was not tested firsthand, and how a recommendation may earn a commission.

How we research products

Our guides draw on product specifications and information published by manufacturers, product documentation, retailer listings, and other publicly available research. We compare practical factors such as workflow, features, price positioning, learning curve, and the type of home coffee setup a product may suit.

Important: Brewcraft has not physically tested every product discussed. Unless an article explicitly says otherwise, our recommendations are research-based and should not be read as hands-on lab tests or personal ownership reviews.

How we choose recommendations

We aim to explain who a product is for, where it makes sense, and what to consider before buying. We consider published features and specifications, ease of use, workflow, learning curve, price and value, limitations, compatibility, and any additional equipment that may be needed. Rankings are editorial judgments based on available research—not a guarantee that one product is best for everyone.

Prices and availability

Prices, promotions, product configurations, and availability can change without notice. Prices shown are approximate reference points unless an article clearly says they were checked live. Confirm current price, specifications, warranty, and seller details with the retailer or manufacturer before purchasing.
