De’Longhi Stilosa Review: Is This Budget Espresso Machine Worth It?

The De’Longhi Stilosa EC260BK is a compact, entry-level pump espresso machine with simple dial controls and a manual milk frother. Its appeal is the low starting cost and small footprint, but buyers should understand the limits of a basic setup before choosing it.

Best for: budget-conscious beginners who want a compact machine for occasional espresso and milk drinks, and are comfortable using pre-ground coffee or buying a separate grinder.

What stands out

Strengths

  • Compact footprint and relatively light 8.6 lb body.
  • Simple dial control with no complicated menu.
  • Stainless-steel boiler and manual milk frother.
  • Can use ESE pods with the appropriate single-cup filter.

Trade-offs

  • No built-in grinder; fresh-ground espresso requires a separate grinder.
  • Basic manual milk frothing takes practice and is less convenient than automatic systems.
  • Entry-level accessories and pressurized baskets are aimed at ease of use rather than extensive espresso experimentation.
  • Small, basic workflow is not ideal for preparing several milk drinks back-to-back.

What is it like to use?

The Stilosa keeps the process straightforward: prepare the coffee, lock in the portafilter, start the shot, and use the manual frother when making a milk drink. The machine does not grind beans for you, so you will need pre-ground coffee or a separate grinder. Its simple controls are approachable, but getting repeatable espresso still depends on grind, dose, tamping, and fresh beans.

Who should buy it?

Choose the Stilosa if the purchase price matters most and you want a basic introduction to espresso-style drinks. It is a reasonable starter option for modest use, especially if you already have a grinder or plan to use ESE pods. If you want a more capable platform for dialing in espresso and improving your technique, compare the Breville Bambino or a machine with a stronger accessory ecosystem.

Our bottom line

The Stilosa is best understood as a low-cost starting point, not a long-term enthusiast machine. Its compact size and simple operation are appealing, but the manual workflow and lack of a built-in grinder mean the full setup may cost more than the machine alone.
