Gaggia Classic E24 Review

The Gaggia Classic E24 takes a more traditional approach than the compact Breville machines. Its lead-free brass boiler and 58 mm stainless-steel filter holder appeal to buyers who want a familiar, hands-on espresso workflow.

Best for: Enthusiasts who want a traditional manual workflow, a 58 mm portafilter, and room to develop technique.

What stands out

This review is based on published manufacturer specifications and product documentation. Brewcraft has not conducted hands-on laboratory or long-term testing of this machine.

Strengths

  • Lead-free brass boiler and upgraded lead-free brass group, according to Gaggia.
  • 58 mm professional-style stainless-steel filter holder.
  • Professional-style stainless-steel steam wand for manual milk texturing.
  • Solenoid valve and traditional rocker-button controls.

Trade-offs

  • The single-boiler design means brewing and steaming require more workflow management than a machine with separate boilers.
  • Milk texturing is fully manual and takes practice.
  • No built-in grinder; a separate grinder is essential for a good setup.
  • The traditional controls and manual descaling/maintenance routines may feel less convenient to beginners.
