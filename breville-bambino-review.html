Breville Bambino Review

The Bambino keeps the essentials simple: a compact footprint, fast heat-up, and manual control over milk texture. It is a sensible entry point if you want to learn espresso without paying extra for automatic milk steaming.

Best for: Beginners who want a compact espresso machine and are happy to steam milk manually.

What stands out

This review is based on published manufacturer specifications and product documentation. Brewcraft has not conducted hands-on laboratory or long-term testing of this machine.

Strengths

  • ThermoJet system reaches extraction temperature in about 3 seconds, according to Breville.
  • 54 mm portafilter and single- and dual-wall filter basket compatibility.
  • PID temperature control and low-pressure pre-infusion.
  • Manual steam wand gives you control over milk texture and latte-art practice.

Trade-offs

  • Milk steaming is manual, so expect a learning curve if you mainly make cappuccinos or lattes.
  • No built-in grinder; budget for a capable espresso grinder.
  • The 54 mm accessory ecosystem differs from standard 58 mm setups.
