How to choose the right machine

A good purchase is about the complete setup—not just the espresso machine price.

1. Leave room in your budget for a grinder

Freshly ground coffee and a grinder that can make fine, consistent adjustments matter for espresso. The Bambino, Bambino Plus, and Gaggia E24 do not include a grinder. Factor that into your budget before comparing them with an all-in-one machine.

2. Be honest about how much work you want to do

Manual machines reward practice. Automatic milk texturing can make daily lattes easier, while a traditional machine gives you more direct control but asks you to learn more steps.

3. Think about your most common drink

If you mostly drink straight espresso, prioritize grinder quality and control. If you make milk drinks every day, the steam wand and milk workflow may matter just as much as the espresso side.

4. Check dimensions and maintenance

Measure counter depth and cabinet clearance. Also consider water-tank access, cleaning routines, descaling, replacement parts, and warranty support.

One last thing: don't buy by pump-bar number.

A box advertising 15 or 20 bar does not mean the coffee is brewed at that pressure. Focus instead on grinder quality, temperature consistency, basket design, and whether the workflow suits you.
